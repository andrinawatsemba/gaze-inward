    How to practice

    Following your breath

    Sit comfortably. Let your attention rest on the feeling of breathing: the air at your nose, or your chest or belly rising and falling. Your eyes can be open or closed. When your mind wanders, which is normal, notice it and gently return to the breath.

    Soft gaze with open eyes

    Keep your eyes open and let them rest on one steady point, without staring hard. Let the edges of what you see stay soft. Some people find this easier than closing their eyes, especially if closing them makes their thoughts feel louder. In this app, the circle is the point to rest your gaze on.

    How the camera mode works

    The app reads your breathing rate with your camera for about 35 seconds, then switches the camera off and guides you at a pace close to your own. It checks again a few times during the session.

    A note

    Different practices suit different people. This app is for wellness only. It does not treat or improve any health condition. If a practice makes you feel uncomfortable, stop. If something is troubling you, talk to someone you trust or a health professional.
